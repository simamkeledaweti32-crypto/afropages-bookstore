### Business Concept
Afropages Bookstore sells books that showcase Cape Town's culture and history. It targets:
- Local students and tourists
- Diaspora in UK/USA wanting to teach kids about home
- International universities studying African heritage
- Cooking enthusiasts wanting Cape Malay recipes

### Features
- Secure Login (admin@afropages.co.za / admin123)
- Real-time Stock Management (12 → 11 deduction)
- Low Stock Alerts and Out of Stock handling
- Add to Cart / Reset Stock
- Shopping Cart with Quantity Update
- Shipping Options: Paxi & DHL
- Checkout with Transaction Receipt
- Search Function
- Mobile Responsive
